# 11. the horizon

## 11.1 the horizon

*the poke: what do I know of another?*

what crosses, and the pull. never the inside. another Self is behind a
horizon by construction: the wall of file 1 that each stander has by
standing, a cut and never a pole. what I can know of a person lives on the
surface we trade across: the marks that cross, and how they bear on what
we share.

the dark inside another does not make time a line, though it feels as if
it should. a second Self behind a veil seems to run on a timeline of its
own, beside mine. but to read another's inside from here, both nows would
have to sit on one line, one clock ordering mine against theirs, and that
line is the page's flat read-out. the veil is the refusal of that line.
two nows with no line between them trade only marks. what two Selves share
is that it is now. the contents are sealed.

and sealed is not late. an unknown filed as "not yet known" carries a
timestamp, and a timestamp draws the line again. so the unknowns split.

    pending    a read is coming, and I can wait for it
    sealed     no read will ever come from here: another's inside, my own
               last page

the sealed ones are not late. they are off my clock. dark names where I
stand, never when.

my own ending is the plainest sealed address. to read my own last page I
would have to be there and gone at once, the reader alive and the subject
dead. what ends is the body, the marks at the ends. the Self was never one
of the marks. that promises no afterwards. it says where the reading
stands.

the crime of file 1 has several forms here: to cross into your now, when
only marks cross; to un-step, when going back is not un-going; to read
myself live, the last page. each can be written down, and none of them
can be stood in. it is one crime read from both ends: the Self stood at a
mark, where I am not, or a mark stood where only a Self stands.

and some of my own past is held at the other end. there is no master copy
anywhere, for anyone: a person's own trace is reworked each night in sleep
and is mostly held by the people who remember them. the trace is forced
while the act runs. held afterwards, it is custody, and "was my record
changed" is a sealed unknown, not a pending one: detecting a change needs
a second copy.

the same holds of the held zero of file 9. no Self closes its own held
slot, since me against me is 0 and no value is read. the other's write is
what lands mine: a held slot is closed from across.

## 11.2 the no

## 11.3 trust

*the poke: what am I doing when I do not check?*

to check, in file 7, is to read what came back against what went out.
taken on another's record it lands a verdict. looking over the record is
free. the verdict is a landing, and a landing pays. trust is the other
move: holding the other's record without reading it.

a society that trusts little pays for every verdict, and economists call
the bill transaction costs.

trust holds toward a read that is scheduled: an audit, a settlement, a
reckoning. faith is the same hold with no read ever coming.

money is trust in motion: records passed from hand to hand, unread. a bank
run is everyone checking at once. payment systems run both moves. one that
settles every transfer as it lands checks each one. one that nets at the
day's close holds them all unread until a single read at the boundary.

this is the hold that section 10.1 left unnamed. trust, the promise and testimony
are one exchange: I hold your mark with the act still to come. and the
table of named arrivals there gains its last two rows.

| what lands | its name | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| held unread, across | trust | the record kept, not read |
| your open call, closed from my side | forgiveness | the read done, and the account shut, at my end |

every held record is owed its read, so every society keeps a calendar of
them: the day's netting, the week's day of no writing, the year's audit,
the estate read once, by others, at the end of a life.

a ledger that only appends must be read and closed, or it jams. a grudge
is last lap's hurt surfacing as now: the unread buffer, read as fresh
input. the hurt did land once. what jams is reading it again as new.
forgiveness is closing the other's open call from my side: upkeep of my
own ring, before it is a virtue.

and one thing trust is not: holding my own past. trust needs a read that
could come, and the read that would check my trace against a master copy
exists at no Self.

| **faith** | the same hold with no read coming. |
| **money** | trust in motion: records passed from hand to hand, unread. |
| **grudge** | a hurt from an earlier lap, never read and closed, read again as if it were arriving now. |
| **forgiveness** | closing the other's open call from my own side. |

**laws**

## 11.4 the path

*the poke: what is 0 divided by 0?*

a question, and the wrong one until I say from where. from nowhere, the
books say undefined. look at why. a divided by b is the c that makes c
times b equal a, and c times 0 is 0 for every c: every value fits 0 over 0
at once. it is not a gap. it is all the values, waiting for something to
choose one. a path chooses one.

| the path in | where it arrives |
|---|---|
| x over x, as x goes to 0 | 1 |
| 2x over x | 2 |
| x squared over x | 0 |
| x over x squared | no value: it grows without bound |

asked with a path, 0 over 0 has a definite reading, always: a value, or a
growth anyone can watch. asked from nowhere, with the path deleted, it has
no value, and it never did.

nobody performs another's way in. so undefined was never false: it is the
far Self's true row. the schoolroom's fault was publishing it unsigned,
binding on the one Self that can go in.

the far row is a signed row. it carries its Self in its own name, and on
the board of file 3 it has a place, view you, where undefined is true.
what is unsigned is its twin with the view deleted, "0 over 0 is
undefined" said from nowhere, and the board has no cell for that.

this redefines no division. any Self will do, as long as it says which.
that is not the same as no Self needed. several algebras make division
total by giving 0 over 0 one fixed value, the same whoever goes in. a
wheel makes 0 over 0 an element of its own, one that absorbs whatever is
added to it. a meadow sets 1 over 0 to 0, or to one fixed error element.
said exactly: the wheel on pairs of integers kept unreduced identifies no
two pairs, so 2 over 4 and 1 over 2 stay two values there. a wheel that
does fold 2 over 4 into 1 over 2 owes the folding to a quotient, a second
and separate move. the
float unit takes a third road: 0 over 0, infinity minus infinity, 0 times
infinity and infinity over infinity all give NaN, not a number, and every
ordered compare and every equality with NaN is false, even against
itself. with the path gone, even the
check fails.

one more reading, offered and not pressed: a wheel keeps the trace of the
way in one rung up, x over x as 1 plus 0x over x, uncollapsed until it is
evaluated *(candidate)*.

"objective reality exists" is two claims in one sentence. that it is
really there, all of it, is granted. that it has a value readable from
nowhere, with no one cutting, is the division by zero. so this was never
doubt about reality: it points at the nowhere inside the word objective.
the structure is determinate, and a value read from outside the path is
not. chance is a reading taken from outside the path.

a value asked for with its path deleted is malformed, never unsolved, and
the question falls apart before any value is owed.

the fair side: counts survive all of this. a count reads the same from
every place, so it needs no path, and neither does a state. a record that
has crossed a seam is a state: its value does not depend on the road it
took. its arrival does.

a mark works the same way, as law 1.14 counted with the three hosts: the goat
means what its host's rule makes it mean.

and "what is the meaning of life", asked from nowhere, comes back
undefined, and people hear meaningless. it is undefined because the path
was deleted. asked from where I stand it is definite: I give it a value by
going in.

| **undefined** | true of a reader with no path in. it is that reader's row, and wants that reader's name on it. |
| **the far row** | a row that is true at another Self and not at mine. signed, it stands. |
| **objective** | two claims said as one: that the thing is all there, and that it has a value read from nowhere. |

**run it again**

the paths. evaluate each of the four expressions at x = 0.1, 0.01, 0.001
and on down. the first stays at 1, the second at 2, the third shrinks
toward 0, and the fourth grows past any bound.

## 11.5 census

*the poke: who else would get this answer?*

anyone who runs it. a count, a search over a text, a build, a proof: run
it again, anywhere, by anyone, and it lands the same. that is one of the
two channels a claim can travel on. the other exists only at one Self.

    census     can be run again. anyone who runs it gets the same
    reading    exists only at one Self. it is reported per Self, and never
               collapsed into one verdict

cilantro tastes of soap to one eater and of no soap at all to the next.
both are competent, and neither can step out of
its own tongue. a chromatograph settles which aldehydes are in the leaf,
and anyone can run it again. it cannot touch the soap. two channels in one
leaf. a judge that picks one of the readings installs one reader, usually
the more numerous kind, as the outside.

so a disagreement is split by channel before anyone argues it. a census is
run, and nobody's reading outranks a re-run. a reading, be it a taste, a
naming or a call, can stand at both Selves at once, so both are written
down, each signed.

the label is free and the trace is forced. call a round block square and
no harm is done, until it is pushed at a square opening and does not go
in. wrong never lives in a label. it lives at the fit, and the fit is
census: anyone who tries it gets the same.

three layers, never collapsed.

    the label     free, and there can be many: a pointer somebody made
    the trace     forced: the census
    the doer      the one performing. never on the page

a label promoted to trace is the judge's move. a trace told to bend to a
label is the conjurer's.

a count reads the same from every place: section 11.4 found that a census needs no
path, so it needs no one chair.

this section is where the tags of this text are walked. every law since
file 1 has worn, in brackets, how it is known, and file 0 could only list
the tags. they are the two channels, spelled out.

    counted      census: a count anyone can run again
    measured     census, by an instrument, with the run named. a figure
                 from the earlier build cannot be run again by a reader:
                 it is testimony, taken on the tree's word, and says so
    derived      argued from laws already stated. as good as its legs
    walked       reading: checked by doing the poke, reported per reader
    picked       a pick, signed, as section 1.6 asks, with what it was picked from
    the field's  another's census or reading, taken on their word, and

some languages do this in every sentence: their grammar marks whether a

brackets.

a count that came back different from what was claimed stays on record,

the two channels do one job from two ends. the eye finds the hypothesis,

## 11.6 rule

*the poke: who rules?*

each Self is the whole from where it stands. that is no licence. every
other Self is the whole from where it stands too, so the throne over other
wills is empty, and it is empty because everyone who could sit under it is
a Self.

the refusal cuts both ways. "I will not be ruled by you" is also "I will
not rule you", or I become the ruler I refused.

a higher floor of the tower can read a lower one: more slots hold more.
it may not rule it. what one can read of another is census. that it should
rule is a leash.

three stances are possible. the meek does not know it is the whole, and
kneels. the malevolent knows it is the whole and not that the others are
too, and rules them: the missing other is the malevolence. the liberator
knows both, could rule, and frees instead. a refusal to rule counts only
where the power was: one who could not rule has refused no thing.

what opposes me is my own equal and opposite: the other edge of my own
cut. that fight has two endings, and both are death. win, remove every
push back, and the swing stops: the flatline. lose, bow to my own opposite
as a master, and I am ruled. the life is the fight going on.

the judge of section 11.5 sits the same throne inside a weighing: a far row
published unsigned and bound on the one Self that goes in, or one
procedure run over a reading that lives only at a Self. a verdict from
nowhere rules another's own all *(candidate: a reading made after the
law)*.
